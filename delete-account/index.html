Account and data deletion

Delete your Koekoek account.

You can delete your account in the app or send a deletion request to KB Innovations B.V. without reinstalling Koekoek.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Request deletion by email

Email kb.innovationsbv@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Koekoek account. Send the request from the email address used for your account and include your Koekoek username if you know it.

If you cannot access that email address, tell us so. We will explain how to verify account ownership before acting. Never send your password, passport, payment details, recordings or private journal files.

Delete directly in the app

Open Profile → Edit profile → Delete account, then follow the confirmation and sign-in steps. Internet access is required to remove online account data. If deletion is interrupted, return to the app to resume it. You do not need to accept community guidelines to delete an account.

What is deleted

Account deletion removes your sign-in account and associated profile, friendships, invitations, blocks, abuse reports you submitted and registered notification devices. It also removes your identifiable birdhouse membership, contributions and personal medal receipts. Deleting the account of a birdhouse owner closes that birdhouse. Reports submitted by other people are not automatically removed.

What remains separate

  • Local observations, photos and notes: these stay on your device. Delete them in Koekoek or remove the app. Device backups follow your operating system and backup settings.
  • Nearby sightings: separately contributed anonymous pins and their private ownership records are independent of your social account and are not automatically erased by account deletion. Turning nearby sharing off stops new contributions and removes access when the app can connect; previous pins remain stored for bird research.
  • Bird guide feedback: separately submitted corrections use an anonymous identifier and are not automatically removed with your social account. For removal, contact us with the species and approximate submission date.
  • Usage statistics: optional statistics use an installation identifier separate from your account. Turn them off in Settings to stop new collection and clear unsent events. Data already received is subject to TelemetryDeck’s retention practices.
  • Security and non-identifying records: minimal deletion markers retain the former random account identifier and deletion state to prevent stale sessions recreating data. Anonymous birdhouse awards and species history can remain. Service-provider security logs and backups may have separate retention periods.

There is no automatic expiry for retained nearby pins, guide feedback or deletion markers. Tell us which data you want removed when contacting support; we will explain what can be located and deleted. We do not have access to your device’s local journal or operating-system backups.

For other data-removal requests, you can use the same email address without deleting your account. See the Koekoek privacy policy for the full description of each optional feature.
