Community guidelines

A welcoming place for birders.

These rules apply to the content you share in Koekoek, including profiles, birdhouse names and nearby sightings.

Version: 28 November 2026 · Provided by KB Innovations B.V.

Before you share

By accepting these guidelines, you agree to follow them when sharing in Koekoek. You can keep listening, browsing the field guide and using your local collection without sharing.

Keep the community safe and respectful

  • Do not share harassment, threats, hateful content or content that promotes violence.
  • Do not share sexual content or any content that exploits, endangers or sexualizes children.
  • Do not share illegal, deceptive or fraudulent content, impersonation, or spam.
  • Do not publish someone else’s private information. Consider your own privacy before sharing a location or personal detail.
  • Share only content you have permission to use. Do not fabricate sightings or deliberately misrepresent an identification.

Report a concern or block someone

Use Report profile or Report birdhouse in the app to report inappropriate content. Use Block to stop unwanted contact from a profile. Blocking does not depend on making a report.

You can also contact kb.innovationsbv@gmail.com. Tell us which profile, birdhouse or sighting concerns you and why. Do not send passwords, identity documents or unrelated private journal material.

How we respond

KB Innovations reviews reports and may remove content or restrict accounts that break these rules. A report is a concern for us to assess; it is not an automatic finding against another person. Contact support if you believe a moderation decision is mistaken.

Your choices

Nearby sharing and optional usage statistics have separate choices in the app. These guidelines do not turn either feature on. See our privacy policy for what is shared and retained, or request account deletion.
