Privacy policy

Your field notes stay yours.

Koekoek keeps your field journal on your device. Optional usage statistics help us improve the app; there is no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Koekoek is provided by KB Innovations B.V.. Privacy contact: kb.innovationsbv@gmail.com.

Local-first data

Using Koekoek for listening, the field guide and your local journal does not require an account. Bird-sound recordings, observation photos, optional capture locations, notes and saved observations are stored locally on your device. Koekoek does not upload these records or media. Optional sharing of broad sighting areas, friend profiles and usage statistics each have a separate choice.

Permissions

Professor Koekoek explains microphone and foreground location access at first launch. You can choose either permission separately or continue. Recording begins when you start listening. Optional foreground location access can attach your phone’s location, fix time and accuracy to that recording. Camera or photo-library access is used only when you choose to add a photo. Denying location or photo access does not prevent you from browsing the field guide or listening without a saved location.

Optional birding reminders

Turn on notifications in Profile → Settings enables morning encouragement and weekly streak reminders together. Koekoek requests notification permission only when you choose to enable or test them. The phone schedules these reminders locally; no push token, reminder preferences or private journal records are sent to a notification service. You can change the reminder times or switch all notifications off together; the short bird whistle is included. Phone sound, Focus and battery settings can affect delivery. Morning encouragement continues even after the weekly goal is complete.

Network connections

Bird recognition runs on-device. Apple Maps may load a basemap over the network, and optional bird-call playback loads source-checked audio from Wikimedia Commons, iNaturalist, the National Park Service, Tierstimmenarchiv or Japan’s National Museum of Nature and Science after you tap play. These audio hosts receive the requested file URL and normal connection information, such as your IP address; Koekoek does not send your microphone recordings or observation coordinates with the request. Opening a source or credit link uses your browser. These providers process requests under their own privacy policies. Local records and the observation list remain available when map tiles or optional audio cannot load.

Vrijwillig vindgebieden delen

Delen staat standaard uit. Kies je ervoor, dan draagt Koekoek bij nieuwe bevestigde waarnemingen de soort, de maand en een grof vindgebied bij aan de buurtkaart en aan vogelonderzoek. In Nederland is zo’n gebied ongeveer 11 bij 14 km. Je exacte vindplaats, opnames, foto’s en profielnaam blijven buiten deze meldingen. Je kunt ook zelf het gebied van je laatste geschikte waarneming delen.

Na je eerste bijdrage zie je welke soorten in en rond dat gebied zijn gemeld. Firebase koppelt je laatste bijdrage aan een aparte anonieme toegangscode. Andere deelnemers zien de samengevoegde meldingen. Zet je delen uit via Kaart of Profiel → Instellingen, dan sluit de buurtkaart meteen. Zodra er verbinding is, laat de app de persoonlijke toegangsregistratie verwijderen. Eerder samengevoegde soortmeldingen blijven beschikbaar.

Optional nearby sightings and bird research

In versions with the nearby map, sharing is off by default. If you turn it on, each new confirmed bird sighting with a recording location contributes a species, calendar month and broad grid area to Firebase. Cells are 0.1° of latitude by 0.2° of longitude, about 11 by 14 km in the Netherlands. You can also explicitly contribute the area of your latest eligible sighting to get started. Exact coordinates, dates within the month, names, audio, photos and notes stay out of these reports.

Participants can see species reported in and around the last area they contributed. Reports of the same species in one area and month merge. The combined records can support bird research. Firebase uses a separate anonymous identifier to manage access, independent of your friend profile and statistics identifier. It keeps your latest contribution privately with this identifier. Other participants see only area-level reports. Firebase receives normal connection information, including the IP address.

Switch off sharing in Map or Profile → Settings to close the nearby map and stop new contributions. The app requests removal of your private access record when connected. Previously combined species reports stay available to other contributors.

Optional usage statistics

Usage sharing is off until you agree. When enabled, Koekoek sends events to TelemetryDeck when an on-device listening analysis completes, an accepted sighting is saved, a new species is discovered, or a badge is earned. Versions supporting invitation statistics also report when you create or accept a friend invitation, with the method (link or username). The service receives no invitation codes, account identifiers, usernames or recipient details. Events include their time, listening duration and whether a result had candidates, the acceptance method, badge type, app version, iOS or Android platform, a random installation identifier and a random event identifier. Bird species, microphone audio, photos, GPS coordinates, notes, file paths and observation identifiers are not sent.

TelemetryDeck receives your IP address to handle the network request and salts and hashes the installation identifier before storing events. The identifier is specific to this app installation and is not an advertising identifier. We use these statistics to understand feature use, not for advertising, cross-app tracking or selling data. No Koekoek account is needed. Counts represent reported actions and installations, not independently verified birds or unique people.

You can allow or decline sharing at first use and change your choice in Profile → Settings → Share usage statistics. Only new actions after consent are eligible; existing journal history is not uploaded. Switching sharing off stops new collection, cancels any in-progress upload where possible, and clears unsent events and the local analytics identifier. A request already received by TelemetryDeck cannot be recalled from the app.

While enabled, up to 1,000 unsent events are kept locally for up to 30 days and sent when the app is active and a connection is available. The free TelemetryDeck plan makes the latest three months available for queries; older received events may remain in the provider’s cold storage. This is not a promise of deletion after three months. Provider handling and rights are described in TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy.

Optional profiles and friends

In versions with friends enabled, you can choose to create an account with an email address and password. Google Firebase Authentication handles sign-in, email verification and password-reset messages. Firebase also receives connection information such as your IP address. Your email address is not shown to other Koekoek users.

Creating or saving your shared profile stores its display name, exact username, short bio, bird avatar, discovered species identifiers, total accepted-sighting count and earned badge identifiers in Google Cloud Firestore, configured in the Netherlands (europe-west4). Other verified users can find your basic profile by exact username or view it if they already have its identifier. Only accepted friends can see your shared collection and badges. These are user-confirmed discoveries, not independently verified identifications.

Shared discoveries are a snapshot from the device where you save the profile or select Update shared discoveries. They are not a cloud backup of your journal. Choosing a bird avatar is limited to discovered species on that device. Recordings, photos, precise or approximate locations, observation dates and notes are not included in this snapshot.

For shared profiles, Koekoek also updates a compact weekly streak summary while you use the app: the number of qualifying weeks, this week’s progress from zero to two spotting days, and week boundaries used to expire outdated progress. Accepted friends can see this summary. A shared streak is calculated from the overlap of both friends’ qualifying weeks, starting with the week they became friends. Individual spotting dates, times and locations are not uploaded for streaks. Streak reminder receipts stay on your device.

Friend requests, relationships, blocks, random invitation codes, creation times and acceptance state are stored to provide the feature. An invitation is private, single-use and valid for seven days unless revoked sooner. You choose where to send it through your device’s share menu; Koekoek does not upload your address book. Recipients and your chosen messaging service receive the content you share.

You can remove friends, block profiles and revoke invitations in Your flock. Delete account removes your online authentication account, profile, shared discoveries, relationships and invitations after you confirm your password. If deletion is interrupted, return to the app to finish it. Local observations remain on your device. A minimal security record containing the former random account identifier, deletion state and update time remains to prevent a still-valid session from recreating deleted data. It contains no email, name, bird discoveries or friendships. Signing out alone does not delete your online profile.

Google processes data for Firebase under its applicable service terms. Authentication, email delivery, security logs and service operations are not limited to the Firestore database region. Provider security logs and backups may have separate retention. See Firebase privacy and security information. Contact us for access or deletion requests that cannot be completed in the app.

Retention and deletion

Your observations remain on the device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting an observation removes that local record and its app-managed attachments according to the app’s deletion flow. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and backup settings.

Children

Local listening and browsing do not require names, email addresses or an account. The optional friends feature requires an email address and a profile. Usage statistics remain a separate, optional choice and are off by default.

Changes

If Koekoek changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.
