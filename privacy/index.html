Privacy policy

What happens to your data?

Koekoek keeps your field journal on your device. Optional usage statistics help us improve the app; there is no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Koekoek is provided by KB Innovations B.V.. Privacy contact: kb.innovationsbv@gmail.com.

Local-first data

Using Koekoek for listening, the field guide and your local journal does not require an account. In the updated journal, microphone clips are temporary and removed after recognition. The app also cleans up clips saved by older versions while keeping their observation records. Observation photos, optional capture locations, notes and saved observations are stored locally on your device. Full journal records and photos stay on your device. Optional nearby sightings, public profiles, birdhouses and usage statistics have separate sharing choices, described below.

Permissions

Professor Koekoek explains microphone and foreground location access at first launch. Koekoek requires microphone permission to finish initial setup and recognise bird sounds. You can leave location off. Recording begins when you start listening. Optional foreground location access can attach your phone’s location, fix time and accuracy to that recording. Camera or photo-library access is used only when you choose to add a photo. Denying location or photo access does not prevent you from browsing the field guide or listening without a saved location.

Optional birding reminders

Birding reminders in Profile → Settings → Notifications enables morning encouragement, lunchtime walks and weekly streak reminders together. Koekoek requests notification permission only when you choose to enable or test them. The phone schedules these reminders locally; no push token, reminder preferences or private journal records are sent to a notification service. You can change the reminder times or turn off local reminders. New versions include a two-note cuckoo call. Phone sound, Focus and battery settings can affect delivery. Morning encouragement continues even after the weekly goal is complete.

Optional invitation notifications

When you allow notifications, flock invitations, acceptances and birdhouse invitations are enabled by default. You can turn invitation notifications off separately in Profile → Settings → Notifications. An existing explicit choice to turn them off is kept. Koekoek verifies friendships with Firebase and birdhouse invitations with its garden service. Open invitations remain available in Inbox even when notifications are off. Its Cloudflare Worker receives the account identifiers involved and processes the Firebase sign-in token to verify the request. If you enable delivery on a device, the Worker stores its Expo push token and selected language for your account. Expo and Apple or Google deliver the notification. The message contains no journal entries, friend names, recordings or location. The notification includes account and invitation identifiers so tapping it opens the correct invitation. Unsent birdhouse alerts are retried for up to 24 hours. You can switch invitation notifications off for this device. Deleting your account removes its registered devices and delivery records.

Network connections

Bird recognition runs on-device. Apple Maps may load a basemap over the network, and optional bird-call playback loads source-checked audio from Wikimedia Commons, iNaturalist, the National Park Service, Tierstimmenarchiv or Japan’s National Museum of Nature and Science after you tap play. These audio hosts receive the requested file URL and normal connection information, such as your IP address; Koekoek does not send your microphone recordings or observation coordinates with the request. Opening a source or credit link uses your browser. These providers process requests under their own privacy policies. Local records and the observation list remain available when map tiles or optional audio cannot load.

Optional nearby sightings and bird research

Your personal map uses the phone’s saved recording location. Sharing is off by default. In the three-day version, fresh optional consent enables sharing saved sightings with a location from today and the previous two calendar days, including uncertain identifications. Your phone moves each shared point by at most 250 metres from the saved spot. In versions with sighting times, participants see the species, recording day, local recording time to the minute and uncertainty. Your name, exact recording coordinates, audio, photos and notes remain private. Earlier consent to sharing only a day, month or broad area does not enable time sharing; you choose again. Times use the recording location’s timezone. Older pins without a recorded time show “Time unavailable”; no time is inferred.

While sharing is enabled, the map shows today, yesterday and the day before. Larger pins are more recent. Your phone recognizes its own contributions from locally stored upload receipts and gives them a coral rim. This ownership indicator stays on your phone. Firebase stores shared pins, a separate anonymous access ID, and private ownership records used to update a pin. These are independent of your friend profile and statistics identifier. Firebase also receives normal connection information, including your IP address.

Turning sharing off in the map settings or Profile stops new contributions and closes the map immediately. Removal of the access record follows when the app can connect. Previously contributed pins and their private ownership records remain stored, including after pins leave the three-day view. The shared data can support bird research.

The earlier individual-pin version shares the month and can still show older pins. The earlier area-map version shares broad areas of 0.1° latitude by 0.2° longitude. Those participants see combined species and month reports around their last contributed area. Existing reports retain their original access rules. Month-only and area reports are excluded from the three-day view because their recording day is unavailable.

Optional usage statistics

Usage sharing is off until you agree. When enabled, Koekoek sends events to TelemetryDeck when you open or return to the app, an on-device listening analysis completes, an accepted sighting is saved, a new species is discovered, a badge is earned, or a bird reaches Silver, Gold, Diamond or Platinum rank. Versions supporting invitation statistics also report when you create or accept a friend invitation, with the method (link or username). The service receives no invitation codes, account identifiers, usernames or recipient details. Events include their time, listening duration and whether a result had candidates, the acceptance method, badge type or attained rank, app version, iOS or Android platform, a random installation identifier and a random event identifier. Bird species, microphone audio, photos, GPS coordinates, notes, file paths and observation identifiers are not sent.

TelemetryDeck receives your IP address to handle the network request and salts and hashes the installation identifier before storing events. The identifier is specific to this app installation and is not an advertising identifier. We use these statistics to understand feature use, not for advertising, cross-app tracking or selling data. No Koekoek account is needed. Counts represent reported actions and installations, not independently verified birds or unique people.

You can allow or decline sharing at first use and change your choice in Profile → Settings → Share usage statistics. Only new actions after consent are eligible; existing journal history is not uploaded. Switching sharing off stops new collection, cancels any in-progress upload where possible, and clears unsent events and the local analytics identifier. A request already received by TelemetryDeck cannot be recalled from the app.

While enabled, up to 1,000 unsent events are kept locally for up to 30 days and sent when the app is active and a connection is available. The free TelemetryDeck plan makes the latest three months available for queries; older received events may remain in the provider’s cold storage. This is not a promise of deletion after three months. Provider handling and rights are described in TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy.

Bird guide feedback

When you choose to send a correction from a species page, Koekoek stores your message, the species identifier, app language and version, and submission time in Google Cloud Firestore. A separate anonymous Firebase identifier supports delivery retries and abuse limits; it is not your social profile, nearby-sharing identity or analytics identifier. Reports are private to the operator; other app users cannot browse them. We use them to review and improve the guide. Please do not include personal details. Your journal, recordings, photos and location are not attached. Reports remain available for review until the operator removes them; there is no automatic expiry. Contact support with the species and approximate submission date for a removal request.

Optional profiles and friends

You can create an account with an email address and password, or use Google or Apple where those sign-in options are offered. Google Firebase Authentication handles sign-in and creates the one-time links for email verification and password resets. Account emails are delivered by Firebase or Resend, depending on your app version. A sign-in provider supplies your account identifier, email address and available basic profile details to Firebase; Koekoek does not receive your Google or Apple password. Apple lets you use a private relay email address. Firebase also receives connection information such as your IP address. Your email address is not shown to other Koekoek users.

For account emails sent through Resend, our existing Cloudflare service processes your email address, chosen language and one-time action link. Messages are held briefly in a delivery queue and removed after handoff to Resend; delivery retries stop after 15 minutes. Resend processes the address and email content for delivery; its provider logs and backups have their own retention periods. Open and click tracking are disabled. To limit abuse, Cloudflare keeps keyed, non-readable identifiers derived from the email address and connection IP for up to 48 hours, anonymous request timestamps for up to 32 days, and delivery failure categories without addresses or links for up to 48 hours. Passwords are never sent to Resend. See Resend’s privacy policy.

Creating or saving your shared profile stores its display name, exact username, short bio, bird avatar and its species-bound rank medal, discovered species identifiers, total accepted-sighting count and earned badge identifiers in Google Cloud Firestore, configured in the Netherlands (europe-west4). Exact-username invitations expose only a minimal identity: your display name, username and bird avatar. Only accepted friends can read your private bio, shared collection and badges. In versions with levels, your current XP also updates while you use the app and accepted friends can see your derived level. These are user-confirmed discoveries, not independently verified identifications.

While you use a shared profile, your discovered species, sighting count, badges and avatar rank update automatically from this device when they change. Failed updates retry when you return to the app or choose to retry. This is a snapshot from one device, not a combined multi-device collection or a cloud backup of your journal. Choosing a bird avatar is limited to discovered species on that device. Recordings, photos, precise or approximate locations, observation dates and notes are not included in this snapshot.

For shared profiles, Koekoek also updates a compact weekly streak summary while you use the app: the number of qualifying weeks, this week’s progress from zero to two spotting days, and week boundaries used to expire outdated progress. Accepted friends can see this summary. A shared streak is calculated from the overlap of both friends’ qualifying weeks, starting with the week they became friends. Individual spotting dates, times and locations are not uploaded for streaks. Streak reminder receipts stay on your device.

Friend requests, relationships, blocks, random invitation codes, creation times and acceptance state are stored to provide the feature. An invitation is private, single-use and valid for seven days unless revoked sooner. You choose where to send it through your device’s share menu; Koekoek does not upload your address book. Recipients and your chosen messaging service receive the content you share. The invitation page uses a code in the link’s fragment, which is processed in your browser and is not sent to the website host in the page request. The host still receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address.

You can remove friends, block profiles and revoke invitations in Your flock. Delete account removes your online authentication account, profile, shared discoveries, relationships and invitations after you confirm your identity with your password or sign-in provider. If deletion is interrupted, return to the app to finish it. Local observations remain on your device. A minimal security record containing the former random account identifier, deletion state and update time remains to prevent a still-valid session from recreating deleted data. It contains no email, name, bird discoveries or friendships. Signing out alone does not delete your online profile.

Google processes data for Firebase under its applicable service terms. Authentication, email delivery, security logs and service operations are not limited to the Firestore database region. Provider security logs and backups may have separate retention. See Firebase privacy and security information. Contact us for access or deletion requests that cannot be completed in the app.

Public profiles and worldwide rankings

In versions with worldwide rankings, profiles start private, including existing accounts. The Public profile switch shares your display name, username, bird avatar and its rank medal, bio, current XP and derived level with signed-in Koekoek users. These data are stored by the existing Cloudflare service and used for the worldwide leaderboard. Public does not mean an indexed website profile. You can browse the board with a private profile. Your full collection, journal, recordings, photos, notes and locations are not made public by this switch.

Turning the switch off removes your profile from subsequent public reads after the server confirms the change. A failed or offline request cannot confirm that the change was saved. Accepted friends and current birdhouse members can still see your level and XP. Removing a friend does not remove that person's separate house-member access when you remain in the same house. Blocking prevents direct profile access and invitations between the blocked accounts; it does not automatically remove anyone from a birdhouse.

Optional birdhouses

Creating or joining a birdhouse shares your identity, level, XP, collected weekly medals and derived house contributions with its current members, up to six people. The joining screen shows the member audience. This does not make your profile public or automatically add friends. Cloudflare stores the house name, ownership, invitations, membership start, decoration choices, weekly roster, scores and awards. Each member separately chooses whether the house may join the next weekly competition. When all eligible members agree, other participating users can see the house name and aggregate weekly score, not its member list.

To calculate points, the service receives limited data for eligible real, accepted captures: an opaque contribution key, species identifier, capture time, saved local-time offset, membership and week, and whether a local photo or field note qualifies for a task. It also receives a random installation source identifier and revision counter to reject duplicate or older submissions. Notes, photos, audio and locations are never included. Members see derived scores and task contributions, not these raw capture records. A time-based task can reveal a broad time window to house members. Joining starts at zero; earlier personal XP and captures do not add house points.

Weekly submissions remain provisional through a 24-hour synchronization period. Raw contribution records are removed when that week is finalized; the service retains anonymous house awards and species history. Only current and preceding-week identifiable result summaries are kept, and departure removes that person's identity and open contributions. Personal medal receipts and their count remain private to the account and visible in aggregate to its current house after moving houses. They are removed on account deletion. Later local edits do not normally rewrite finalized awards, and a deletion that has not synced before finalization cannot be known to the service.

You can leave a house at any time. An owner transfers ownership to an accepting member or closes the house. Account deletion first records a resumable deletion state, then removes the public projection, membership, identifiable contributions and medal receipts before completing the remaining account cleanup. Deleting an owner's account closes their house. Minimal security markers retain only the former random account identifier and deletion state to prevent stale sessions recreating data. Cloudflare receives normal connection information and verifies the Firebase sign-in token. Provider security logs and backups can have separate retention; these account features are not journal backups.

Retention and deletion

Your observations remain on the device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting an observation removes that local record and its app-managed attachments according to the app’s deletion flow. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and backup settings.

Children

Local listening and browsing do not require names, email addresses or an account. The optional friends feature requires an email address and a profile. Usage statistics remain a separate, optional choice and are off by default.

Changes

If Koekoek changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.
