Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

You can listen, browse birds and keep your own journal without an account. Create a profile when you want to add friends or join a birdhouse. Microphone access is part of the initial setup; location and sharing are optional.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password?, enter your email address and check your inbox. Check spam too. If you use Google or Apple to sign in, choose the same sign-in button instead.

My verification email hasn’t arrived.

Check the email address shown in the app and your spam folder. You can send the verification email again from that screen. After opening its link, return to Koekoek and choose I’ve verified my email.

How do I accept a friend invitation?

Open the invitation link and choose Open Koekoek. Sign in or create a profile, then accept the invitation. If the app doesn’t open, copy the code from the page and paste it into Your flock → Add friends. Each invitation is for one friend and lasts seven days. Ask for a new one if it has expired or already been used.

What is a birdhouse?

A birdhouse is a group for you and up to five others. Work on weekly challenges, earn decorations and furnish your house together. The weekly competition is optional and needs agreement from every eligible member.

How can I improve a recording?

Move away from traffic and conversation, keep the phone clear of wind and avoid covering the microphone. A nearby, clear call works better than distant or overlapping sounds.

Why do I see several suggestions or no match?

Recognition results are model suggestions, not guaranteed scientific identifications. Quiet audio, overlapping species and unfamiliar sounds can produce several candidates or no match.

Does Koekoek upload my recordings?

No. Recognition runs on your device. New journal entries keep the observation details and discard the microphone clip after review. Tapping the sound in an observation plays an available species reference call. The app also removes microphone clips saved by older versions while keeping their observation records.

Why does Koekoek request my location?

Foreground location is optional and can be attached to a recording so your saved observation appears on your personal map. Denying it does not block listening or the field guide.

How do I unlock the nearby map?

Choose Nearby map and enable sighting sharing. The three-day version shows birds from today, yesterday and the day before. Larger pins are more recent. Your contributions have a coral rim and “My sighting” in their details. Versions with sighting times also show the local recording time in the pin preview and list. You share the species, recording day, recording time to the minute and uncertainty. Older pins without a time show “Time unavailable”. Each public pin is within 250 metres of the saved recording spot. Use the gear to switch sharing off again. Time sharing requests fresh consent if you previously shared only a day, month or broad area.

Why does the map or a bird call need internet?

Apple Maps can load its basemap over the network. Optional example calls stream from source-checked Wikimedia Commons, iNaturalist, the National Park Service or Tierstimmenarchiv recordings when you tap play. Optional usage statistics connect to TelemetryDeck only after you agree in the app. Your local observation list remains available when those services cannot load.

How do I remove an observation?

Open the observation from Map or your saved records and choose the delete action. Removing the last accepted observation for a species returns that species to its undiscovered silhouette.

What should I send to support?

Send the app version, iOS version, device model and reproducible steps. Please do not send private recordings, photos or exact locations unless support specifically asks and you choose to share them.
