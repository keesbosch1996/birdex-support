Frequently asked questions

How can I improve a recording?

Move away from traffic and conversation, keep the phone clear of wind and avoid covering the microphone. A nearby, clear call works better than distant or overlapping sounds.

Why do I see several suggestions or no match?

Recognition results are model suggestions, not guaranteed scientific identifications. Quiet audio, overlapping species and unfamiliar sounds can produce several candidates or no match.

Does Birdex upload my recordings?

No. Recognition runs on your device. Your recordings and journal records remain in local app storage unless you independently choose to share them.

Why does Birdex request my location?

Foreground location is optional and can be attached to a recording so your saved observation appears on your personal map. Denying it does not block listening or the field guide.

Why does the map or a bird call need internet?

Apple Maps can load its basemap over the network. Optional example calls stream from source-checked Wikimedia Commons, iNaturalist, the National Park Service or Tierstimmenarchiv recordings when you tap play. Optional usage statistics connect to TelemetryDeck only after you agree in the app. Your local observation list remains available when those services cannot load.

How do I remove an observation?

Open the observation from Map or your saved records and choose the delete action. Removing the last accepted observation for a species returns that species to its undiscovered silhouette.

What should I send to support?

Send the app version, iOS version, device model and reproducible steps. Please do not send private recordings, photos or exact locations unless support specifically asks and you choose to share them.
